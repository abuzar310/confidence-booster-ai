# Night Cut

The frame is the subject. Everything else is a thin material that floats over it — never a panel that hides the face.

Space is the live camera. Chrome occupies the edges: four L-corners, a glass dock, one saturated mark. Color is scarce. Pink is the drop; indigo is the live state. Nothing else glows.

Scale is phone-first. Rhythm is 8px. Composition is a viewfinder, not a dashboard. Hierarchy is one verb: Drop.

Craft is the product of hours: alignments snap, press answers on the down, glass never stacks on glass, type never shouts in the HUD.

Text is a label, not a paragraph. The cut is the sentence.
